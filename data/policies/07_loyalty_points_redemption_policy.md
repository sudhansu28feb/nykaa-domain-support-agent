# Nykaa Policy: Loyalty-Points Redemption Policy

Nykaa Privé members accumulate reward points on all authenticated purchases across beauty, fashion, and lifestyle verticals. For every ₹100 successfully spent on the platform, customers earn 1 loyalty point, where each loyalty point maintains a redemption valuation of ₹1 INR. Customers can redeem accrued points during checkout once their balance reaches a minimum threshold of 100 reward points. Reward points may be redeemed to settle up to 50% of the total cart value, and unused points remain valid for 12 calendar months from the accrual date.
